Thirukkural is one of the greatest works of Tamil literature, offering timeless wisdom on virtue, society, leadership and love.

1330

1330 Couplets

Thirukkural consists of 1330 couplets, divided into 133 chapters across three major sections — Aram, Porul and Inbam.

01

அறத்துப்பால்

Aram · Virtue

Wisdom about righteousness, character, compassion and living a meaningful life.

02

பொருட்பால்

Porul · Wealth

Teachings about leadership, society, friendship, governance and prosperity.

03

இன்பத்துப்பால்

Inbam · Love

Exploring love, emotions, relationships and the beauty of human connection.
